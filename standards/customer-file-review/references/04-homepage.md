# 04 Homepage / 首页

Source PDF: `../source-pdfs/04-首页填写规范.pdf`

## Homepage Structure

The B2B homepage uses seven sections:

1. Hero
2. Social Proof
3. Products
4. Why Choose Us
5. Cases
6. FAQ
7. CTA

## Required Items

| Section | Requirement | Evidence to Check |
|---|---|---|
| Hero headline | One sentence explaining who the company is, what it offers, and for whom. Any customer-preferred language is acceptable. | Homepage form field or draft copy. |
| Hero visual | 2-3 high-resolution candidate images, ideally at least `1920x1080px`. | Files under `04-首页/hero-images/` or equivalent; dimensions if available. |
| Social proof data | 4-6 real data points with short labels. | Form fields or spreadsheet rows. |
| Certifications for homepage | Core certification badges or scans if used for trust proof. | Files under `04-首页/social-proof/` or brand certificates referenced from module 03. |
| Products section | 3-6 main product categories. | Homepage form or product category materials. |
| Why Choose Us | 4-6 core advantages; each should include a title, support explanation, and visual/data/certification support when available. | Form fields plus files under `04-首页/why-choose-us/` or equivalent. |
| Cases section | Complete selected cases should exist in the case library and include qualified cover images. | Cross-reference module 09. |
| FAQ | 5-8 common pre-order questions relevant to the customer business. | Homepage form fields or FAQ draft. |

## Recommended Items

| Section | Recommendation |
|---|---|
| Hero headline | Keep it concise in the customer's source language; the service team can adapt the final target-language length and SEO wording. |
| Customer logo wall | 6-12 authorized customer logos, PNG transparent or SVG preferred. |
| Why Choose Us support | Use concrete data, factory/process photos, certificates, awards, or screenshots. |
| FAQ | Draw from real inquiry questions; do not force irrelevant generic questions. |

## Review Notes

- Do not require the customer to write SEO-polished target-language copy; rough source-language drafts are acceptable.
- If social proof numbers seem unsupported, mark `需确认` and ask for confirmation rather than accusing.
- If customer logos are present, authorization should be confirmed. Unclear authorization is `需确认`.
- If hero images cannot be dimension-checked, mark image quality `需确认`.
