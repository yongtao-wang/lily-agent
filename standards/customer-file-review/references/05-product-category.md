# 05 Product Category Pages / 产品分类页

Source PDF: `../source-pdfs/05-产品分类页·填写规范.pdf`

## Scope

The customer provides business logic, rough category names/descriptions, and category images in the language they are most comfortable using. The service team handles URL slug, page title, meta description, keyword allocation, target-language adaptation, and SEO.

## Required Items for A-Type Rebuild

| Item | Requirement | Evidence to Check |
|---|---|---|
| Old category URLs | Complete list of old website product category URLs. | Spreadsheet field, document, or URL list. |
| Category main images | Required if old images are low quality or style-inconsistent. | Image files and dimensions if available. |

## Required Items for B-Type New Site

| Field | Requirement | Evidence to Check |
|---|---|---|
| Category name | Product category name in any customer-preferred language. | `05-产品分类【填写表】` or equivalent. |
| Category introduction | 100-200 words describing what the category is, who it serves, and key specification range. | Form/spreadsheet field. |
| Category header image filename | Image filename matching the image folder. | Field plus corresponding image file. |

## Recommended Items

| Item | Recommendation |
|---|---|
| Title suggestion | Optional. Customer may leave blank if they do not have a clear title idea. |
| Category structure | Should match buyer decision logic, not only internal production classification. |

## Image Placement

Each category header image should be placed under a product category image folder such as `05-产品分类页/产品分类图片/` or equivalent. SEO filename formatting is not required from the customer.

## Review Notes

- For unknown project type, mark A/B-specific URL requirements as `需确认`.
- Do not mark missing slug, title, meta, or keywords as a customer gap; those are service-team outputs.
